---
title: Cry For Another
date: 2018-03-30
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

I was just filled with peace,
But now it’s no more.
A burden has found me,
And it comes from the Lord.

I can’t try to explain it,
This turmoil inside.
I feel sick; I feel weak.
There’s nowhere to hide.

It can start as a worry,
Have I done something wrong?
It can then move to anguish,
A depressing new song.

But the Lord, He won’t leave me.
He will show me the way.
For there’s a soul He has chosen,
And He wants me to pray.

I will talk to the Spirit,
I will seek His advice.
He is faithful in council,
He is loving and wise.

He has given an impression,
It’s time now to speak.
There’s no place for judgement,
I can only be meek.

My prayer starts with words,
Sounds my mind understands.
They soon turn to groanings,
Speaking words not of man.

My heart is so heavy,
I begin to travail.
My eyes fill with tears,
I cry and I wail.

The trouble is now lifting,
I am finding relief.
The intercession is over,
I’m now filled with peace.

It’s a gift of great love,
That my Lord had me pray.
Another soul will be blessed;
They’ll be lifted this day.
