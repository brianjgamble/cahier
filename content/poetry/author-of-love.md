---
title: Author of Love
date: 2017-11-30
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

If love is a verb,
It’s an act lost on me.
If love is a noun,
It’s a thing I can’t see.

If love is a person,
It’s someone I can’t find.
If love is a place,
It’s a town far behind.

If love is a direction,
On which road should I turn?
If love is a purpose,
Which way should I yearn?

After all of those thoughts,
Something new comes to mind.
Am I blinded by pain,
That will heal in due time?

Before I can think,
Something comes into sight.
It’s been there all along,
Full of words that bring life.

To experience and live,
A love that is real;
I must surrender my life,
And it starts when I kneel.

I repent of my sins,
Give Him all of my pain.
That man’s name is Jesus;
He’s the truth and the way.
