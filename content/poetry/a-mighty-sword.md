---
title: A Mighty Sword
date: 2014-10-29
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

I carry a great weapon.
It’s quick and it’s sharp.
It gives me great comfort,
And strength in the dark.

In power and sharpness,
There’s no rival you’ll find.
It discerns and it pierces;
This sword is sublime.

Against flesh and blood,
Is not the battle I fight;
Principalities and wickedness,
Well beyond my own might.

My weapon is not steel;
I wield something more.
It’s the sword of the Spirit;
The Word of the Lord.
