---
title: My Strength
date: 2014-10-22
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

There’s something within me;
I can’t quite explain.
A feeling of dread,
A solemn refrain.

I don’t know how it started,
Or why it is there.
It continues to haunt me.
It makes me despair.

I’m willing to fight it,
To stand and engage.
I’m worried my courage
Will leave me dismayed.

It attacks when I’m up.
It attacks when I’m down.
It makes me feel worthless;
This stronghold’s profound.

It keeps me from action;
The worst will I see.
I’m stuck and I’m frozen.
In a rut; set me free.

Behind walls of doubt,
Came whispers of hope.
Gentle but strong;
Words of power spoke.

A spirit of fear,
It’s not what I’ve been given.
A soundness of mind,
Power and love shall be risen.

These words how they lingered,
Like a song in the air.
New courage is rising.
A new hope, it is there.

I renounce this old stronghold,
The fear, and the blame.
I no longer accept you.
Turn and go whence you came.

I humble my heart;
Bend my knees to the floor.
I’ve now found my strength.
It comes from the Lord.
