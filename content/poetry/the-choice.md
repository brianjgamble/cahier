---
title: The Choice
date: 2019-11-05
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

The jaws of death,
They no longer can hold.
They will rip, they will tear.
They will howl and will scold.

The teeth are now rotting,
The jaws aren’t so tight.
The sting has abated,
Are we ready to fight?

We’re so used to losing,
Can there be a new way?
The victory precedes us,
We must trust and obey.

How long will we struggle?
How long do we fear?
The enemy has been broken;
His end does draw near.

How long will we suffer,
And accept all the lies?
How long ’til it’s clear,
That there’s power inside.

Choose life and the blessings,
Not curses and death.
Your choice is important,
You must take what is best.

Live for today,
Say goodbye to the past.
Don’t fret for the future;
Make a stand that will last.

The clock is still ticking,
Your life is a race.
Are you running to finish?
Will you hide in disgrace?
