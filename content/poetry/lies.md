---
title: Lies
date: 2023-06-18
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

Who will hear my cry?
Who will feel my pain?
My strength is almost gone.
This struggle just leads to shame.

Fear is all around,
Anxiety’s at the door.
No matter what I do,
My hope is on the floor.

My trouble has found a home,
Oh when did I let it in?
How could this happen to me?
I’ve forgotten how to win.

The lies are coming fast.
The guilt is shouting too.
Condemnation has arrived;
The truth is far from view.

It’s the only thing he has,
That dirty, wicked snake.
He points a crooked finger,
And condemns you to your fate.

Why do I let this get me?
This fiery attack from hell.
He’s a liar and a thief;
A murderer as well.

The longer that I linger,
Soaking up these wretched lies.
The longer that the torment
Will refuse to run and hide.

It’s easy to forget,
When you feed on guilt and shame.
The enemy’s defeated;
When you’re saved there is no blame.

If I listen to the lies,
It will slowly steal my peace.
I must remember who is strong,
When I know that I am weak.

I’m in the hands of Jesus,
The one who’s filled with love.
He protects me from the enemy,
I’m covered like a glove.
