---
title: Summer's Gone
date: 2017-09-21
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

The smell of the air,
The crispness of the breeze,
My senses awaken to a memory renewed.

A change in the mood,
A spark of new life,
Yesterday’s plans are out of place and gone by.

A shift in the landscape,
The transformation of things green,
The clock is conspiring to shorten the day.

The color of the sky,
A change in the light,
My eyes see the return of an old friend.
