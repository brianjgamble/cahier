---
title: Reset
date: 2019-03-03
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

It’s time to step back,
It’s time for new eyes.
The failures have lingered,
There’s no place I can hide.

The hurts are so deep,
The struggles are now real.
My strength has been waning;
All the pain, will it heal?

Success seems elusive,
The goals out of reach.
The hurdles keep growing,
They get caught in my feet.

The whispers of sadness,
The thoughts of despair.
Opposition is rising.
Can I breathe? Where’s the air?

The past looks so rosy,
Full of promise and hope.
I was once full of vigor,
I could climb any rope.

Looking forward from those years,
To now, where I stand,
It’s a much different place,
It’s a foreign and strange land.

Of the waters of Marah,
I’ve consumed many cups.
It’s clouded my vision,
I’m now in a rut.

My future will be gone,
If I stay without change.
I will weaken my heart,
I will fade, drift away.

My pride is a bully,
It would change how I think.
There is only one answer,
I must muster my strength.

I must look to my Father,
Who awaits with pure love.
He knows that I hurt;
He brings peace like a dove.

If I run to His arms,
I must turn from my past.
I’ll begin a new day,
With a joy that will last.

I will win, I will lose.
I will run, I will fall.
With God by my side,
There’s no climb that’s too tall.
