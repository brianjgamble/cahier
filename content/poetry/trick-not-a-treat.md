---
title: Trick, Not A Treat
date: 2014-10-20
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

It’s not in good fun,
And it’s not make-believe.
There’s something much deeper,
A plan to deceive.

No matter the words,
Or a passion displayed;
Some will not hear
Or stop in dismay.

I pray you will listen,
The truth will be plain.
This night is not harmless,
This eve of Samhain.

It must now be known
This day closed the year.
A celebration by Druids
With witchcraft and fear.

The wearing of masks
And costumes alike,
Hide the true faces
For mischief and fright.

Divination and demons
Are things of this day.
To ignore it’s true purpose
Sends evil your way.

I hope you now see,
I hope you despise.
A day that will worship
The father of lies.
