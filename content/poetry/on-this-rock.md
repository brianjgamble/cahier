---
title: On This Rock
date: 2022-04-18
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

Upon this rock
Is a place that I know.
Where old things will die
And new life will grow.

To give Him my all,
The hopes and the fears,
Is what He will ask,
An act He holds dear.

In the midst of the storm
I can reach out in trust;
To the hem of the garment,
Where evil plans come to dust.

The peace that will follow
Is hard to describe.
It’s a place where your worry
Will fall and subside.

He wants you to seek Him,
Lay it down at His feet.
Open up all your burdens,
See the cross and be meek.

For God loves us dearly,
He traded His life.
To buy us with riches,
To overcome with His might.

So on this rock,
My strength, it will fall.
My relationship will grow;
He is Lord of my all.
