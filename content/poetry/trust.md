---
title: Trust
date: 2017-09-20
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

Learning to trust,
It is tied to my faith.
It is not something earned,
It is not something placed.

It is not from my looking,
For the fruit of the vine.
It’s not from my working,
Toward results that are mine.

It’s not from a comfort,
That keeps me well fed.
Or from walls and a roof,
Where I lay down my head.

There is only one place,
From which real trust proceeds.
That place is through faith,
When I start to believe.
