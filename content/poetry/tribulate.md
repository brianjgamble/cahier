---
title: Tribulate
date: 2017-11-23
author: Brian J. Gamble
---

Oh, why does my peace
Seem to come and then go?
Like the waves on a beach,
Or a late winter snow?

An oppression was lifted;
Confusion pushed aside.
A tormentor was beaten.
No more will it hide.

The battle is still fresh;
There is dust from the wall.
A stronghold was taken,
To the ground it did fall.

Each campaign I will savor,
Every win will I cheer.
I will always be fighting,
Against pain, against fear.

Sometimes it is hard,
To stop wave after wave.
But I cannot give up,
I must sharpen my blade.

Little by little,
Of the ground I will take.
I will not stop fighting,
When my life is at stake.

Satan’s horde will not stop,
Their attack comes from Hell.
Will I stand? Will I conquer?
Or concede at the bell?

Till the day I breathe last,
I will never give up.
For His purpose I was created,
I will drink from the cup.

My bliss is now waining,
The strife it is near.
A demon is now stalking,
Its footsteps I can hear.
